Assignment name  : fprime_pow
Expected files   : fprime_pow.c
Allowed functions: atoi, write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes a strictly positive int and displays its prime
factorization, followed by a newline.

The prime factors are displayed in increasing order, each one only once. When a
factor appears several times, it is followed by '^' and its number of
occurrences. The factors are separated by '*'.

The factorization of 1 is displayed as "1".

If the number of parameters is not 1, the program just displays a newline.

The parameter is always valid: a strictly positive number that fits in an int.

Your program must answer in less than a second, whatever the input.

Examples:

$> ./fprime_pow 360 | cat -e
2^3*3^2*5$
$> ./fprime_pow 17 | cat -e
17$
$> ./fprime_pow 1024 | cat -e
2^10$
$> ./fprime_pow 1 | cat -e
1$
$> ./fprime_pow 2147483647 | cat -e
2147483647$
$> ./fprime_pow | cat -e
$
$>
